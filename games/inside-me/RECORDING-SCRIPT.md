# Inside Me: recording script

Read each numbered line aloud, in order, **without saying the number**.
Leave a clear pause (about 2 seconds of quiet) after every line. That gap is how the
recording gets cut up, so a stumble is fine: pause, then read the line again
and put "REDO" in your upload note, or just say it twice in a row.

Do it in as many takes as you like. Suggested: one take of names, then facts in chunks of about 40.

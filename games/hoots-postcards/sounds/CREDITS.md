# Hellos in Hoot's Postcards

Real people saying hello, from Wikimedia Commons, most of them recorded
for [Lingua Libre](https://lingualibre.org/). For the game each one was
trimmed, made mono and evened out in loudness, once, with ffmpeg; the
adapted clips are shared under the same licence as their originals.

Places with no clip (the Arctic, Scotland, South Africa, Nepal and
Antarctica) have none because no freely licensed recording of their
hello could be found. Their hello is written in a speech bubble, as
every place's is.

        Your job, Director

        You are responsible for helping the students at your Academy learn. The settings below decide how hard their exams are and how much help they get along the way.

        Give a student plenty of support while they are finding their feet, then take it away a bit at a time as they improve. That is where the learning happens. Nothing here is permanent, so change it as often as you like.

        Changes apply to that rider's next exam, on whichever tablet she picks up.

        Rider

        Every rider has her own settings. These follow her name, not this tablet.
